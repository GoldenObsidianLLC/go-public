# Vision and Culture

This section contains approved public-facing material about Golden Obsidian LLC: its purpose, principles, culture, and founder context.

## Canonical company document

- [Company Identity](company-identity.md) - the current review-stage candidate for the canonical overview of the company's mission, vision, engineering philosophy, portfolio structure, and public-claim boundaries.

Once approved, keep the company identity page as the single canonical home for company-wide positioning. Until its status changes from review to published, treat its wording as a candidate public statement and do not silently duplicate it in other sections.

## Intended contents

- Public manifestos and statements of principle.
- Founder biography and approved professional background.
- Cultural practices and the principles that guide collaboration.

## Publishing guidance

Use this section for enduring context rather than product specifications or time-sensitive announcements. Keep claims factual, attributable where appropriate, and approved for public release. Do not publish private contact details, personal information that has not been approved for release, internal deliberations, or confidential operating procedures.

Publish a separate founder biography only when its source text and personal details have been explicitly approved for public release.
