# Vision and Culture

This section contains approved public-facing material about Golden Obsidian LLC: its purpose, principles, culture, and founder context.

## Intended contents

- Company mission, vision, and values.
- Public manifestos and statements of principle.
- Founder biography and approved professional background.
- Cultural practices and the principles that guide collaboration.

## Publishing guidance

Use this section for enduring context rather than product specifications or time-sensitive announcements. Keep claims factual, attributed where appropriate, and approved for public release. Do not publish private contact details, personal information that has not been approved for release, internal deliberations, or confidential operating procedures.

The founder biography and other detailed notes will be added as canonical documents when their source text is available in this repository and ready for publication.
