# Golden Obsidian LLC — Company Identity

> **Sovereign Strength. Governed Intelligence. National Security.**

## Corporate identity

Golden Obsidian LLC is a Houston-based, engineering-led sovereign technology and advanced systems engineering company. It researches, develops, and commercializes systems across Governed Intelligence, spatial computing, human–AI collaboration, and enabling developer infrastructure.

The company is building an interoperable portfolio rather than a single application or a general-purpose software consultancy. Its work combines systems engineering, performance-conscious implementation, governed operation, and disciplined product development.

In Golden Obsidian's vocabulary, **sovereign technology** describes the design objective of preserving meaningful control over data, compute, deployment, system behavior, and operating policy. It is an engineering and governance principle; it is not a claim of legal sovereignty, regulatory approval, or certification.

## Mission

To research, develop, commercialize, and operate high-assurance Governed Intelligence systems, spatial-computing platforms, human–AI collaboration software, and enabling developer technologies through measurable engineering, physical-economic feasibility, evidence-gated technical rigor, and accountable corporate governance.

## Vision

An interoperable ecosystem of intelligent and spatial systems that gives builders and operators durable control, practical agency, and trustworthy tools for complex work.

## Engineering philosophy

Golden Obsidian's operating principles are:

1. **Engineering sovereignty:** favor architectures that preserve operator control, portability, inspectability, and practical deployment choice.
2. **Uncompromising craftsmanship:** value correctness, performance, maintainability, and deliberate design over novelty or unnecessary complexity.
3. **Human agency:** build technology to extend human capability while keeping responsibility and meaningful control with people.
4. **Evidence before assertion:** distinguish tested behavior from hypotheses, prototypes, plans, and marketing language.
5. **Physical-economic feasibility:** account for compute, storage, networking, energy, operational burden, and lifecycle cost—not merely theoretical capability.
6. **Security and privacy by design:** minimize exposure, constrain authority, protect sensitive information, and treat security as an architectural concern.

These principles are decision criteria, not claims that every project has already achieved a particular performance, security, or assurance level.

## Commercial and strategic direction

Golden Obsidian's commercial direction centers on reusable platforms, governed software capabilities, spatial-computing infrastructure, and developer technologies. Enterprise, industrial, institutional, and strategically relevant public-sector use cases may inform product development, subject to technical feasibility, lawful authorization, and evidence-backed readiness.

The phrase **National Security** in the company tagline expresses a strategic area of interest. It does not, by itself, imply a government relationship, contract, export authorization, security clearance, regulatory status, or qualification for defense use.

## Public claims and evidence discipline

Company-wide descriptions belong here; implementation details, release notes, supported configurations, and project-specific license terms belong in each project's authoritative repository.

Public materials must keep these distinctions explicit:

- **Released:** available in a verifiable release or documented service.
- **Preview or prototype:** limited evaluation or development work; not presumed production-ready.
- **Planned:** intended future work, not an existing capability.
- **Verified:** supported by reproducible evidence appropriate to the claim.

Do not publish unsubstantiated throughput, model-architecture, scale, compatibility, safety, security, or cost claims. Do not claim certifications, regulatory compliance, independent validation, partnerships, awards, customer deployments, or financial performance without current, attributable evidence and approval for public release.
