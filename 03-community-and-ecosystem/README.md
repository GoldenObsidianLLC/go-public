# Community and Ecosystem

This section documents the public community around Golden Obsidian LLC and its projects.

## Intended contents

- How to participate, collaborate, and request support.
- Publicly approved supporters, partners, and integrations.
- Awards, recognition, events, and community milestones.
- Community resources and links to official project spaces.

## Publishing guidance

Name an individual, organization, partner, or supporter only when the relationship is accurate and public disclosure is authorized. Attribute awards and recognition to their issuing organizations and link to verifiable sources. Distinguish formal partnerships from informal collaboration, community participation, sponsorship, or general support.

Do not publish private correspondence, personal contact details, confidential agreements, private community messages, or unapproved logos and brand assets.
