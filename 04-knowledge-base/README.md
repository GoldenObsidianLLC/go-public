# Knowledge Base

This section provides practical, reusable information for users, contributors, and community members.

## Intended contents

- Getting-started guides and setup instructions.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Tips, troubleshooting notes, and known limitations.
- Workflows and examples that are approved for public reuse.
- Glossary entries for terminology used across the ecosystem.

## Publishing guidance

Prefer one canonical article for each topic. Link to it from other pages rather than maintaining duplicated instructions. State prerequisites, supported versions, expected outcomes, and known limitations. Mark uncertain or environment-specific steps clearly, and include a tested date when instructions depend on a changing toolchain.

Examples and helper scripts must follow the [licensing policy](../05-governance-and-policies/licensing.md). Do not include secrets, private endpoints, production credentials, or internal troubleshooting records.
